Runa is a private, personal journal. Write down the moments that matter — with photos, location, and an exact date and time — and keep everything synced to your own Nextcloud server.

FEATURES
• Write journal entries with title, body, date/time, and up to 5 photos
• Retroactive entries — log memories from years ago, not just today
• Tag entries and filter your journal by tag
• Photos from gallery or camera, with EXIF rotation correction
• Share images from any app straight into Runa's Write screen
• Read tab grouped by year, month, and day
• Map view (OpenStreetMap) — every entry with a location becomes a pin, no third-party account required
• Nextcloud WebDAV sync (Login Flow v2)
• Local-only mode — no server required
• Biometric app lock
• Dark and light theme, with a warm night map for comfortable evening viewing
• In-app text size control
• Export your journal and photos as a ZIP

PRIVACY
No Runa servers, no accounts, no tracking. Sync is directly to your own Nextcloud instance, or keep everything local-only.
